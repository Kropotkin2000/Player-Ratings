Six-League Player Ratings

Every regular player in the Premier League, Championship, La Liga, Serie A, Ligue 1 and Bundesliga, rated on one scale: the expected-goal difference per 90 minutes he adds over an average Premier League starter in his position.

How to read the ratings

Rating is the xG difference per 90 minutes a player adds compared with an average Premier League starter in his position, so 0 is that average and +0.20 is a fifth of an expected goal a match. Goals a season is the same figure over 38 full matches. ± is the uncertainty: two players closer together than that are not really separated.

Each rating is built from the last two years of matches and has four parts. League level: how strong his league is against the Premier League. Box score: what his own numbers per 90 (xG, xA, passing, defensive actions and so on) are worth for his position. On pitch: how his team's xG for and against moves when he is playing, beyond what his own numbers explain. Finishing: goals above his xG, heavily damped because finishing is mostly luck over short spells.

How the parts combine. The four parts are added up, and then the same total for the average Premier League starter in his position is taken away. That average starter is himself well above zero on the parts, which is why the part columns add up to more than the Rating. Example: a winger with league level −0.09, box score +0.49, on pitch +0.05 and finishing +0.02 totals +0.47; the average Premier League winger totals about +0.13; his rating is +0.34.

Keepers are rated on shot-stopping only (goals prevented against the quality of the shots they faced) and are the least certain position: about ±0.07 each on a spread of about 0.03, so only the two ends of the keeper list mean much.

A league name after a player (for example · Eredivisie) means his rating rests mainly on matches there. Such ratings carry over about as well as a move between two of the six leagues, and get a wider ±. "Out 90 d" is the number of days since his last league match.

Match data: Fotmob. Values: Fotmob's market values, in millions of euros. The ratings are rebuilt every day.
